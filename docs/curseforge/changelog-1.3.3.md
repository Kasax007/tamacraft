# Tamacraft 1.3.3 (Minecraft 26.3)

Bug fixes.

- **Fish Crackers and Pet Kibble** had the same recipe: wheat with a cod or a salmon could give either of them, so one
  of the two could not be crafted that way. Fish Crackers now take two wheat and a cod or a salmon. Pet Kibble stays
  one wheat with meat or fish.

Worlds and pets from 1.3.0 to 1.3.2 load as they are.
